# micro-31 Examples and Boundaries

## Decision Variable
actual outcome versus salient alternative outcome

## Route Signal
Route here when the scene asks for regret, relief, disappointment, or pleasant surprise based on a better-or-worse alternative outcome.

## Hard Boundary
- No counterfactual contrast, no micro-31.
- If guilt, shame, pride, anger, or gratitude depends on fault or moral value, route to moral emotion instead.
- If the focus is emotional expression or suppression, route to display rules or emotion regulation instead.

## Shortcut To Avoid
- Do not label any bad outcome as regret by default.
- Do not confuse disappointment with simple sadness.
- Do not infer relief unless the character is comparing the actual outcome with a worse possible outcome.

## Common Failure Modes
- Answering with a generic positive or negative emotion instead of the counterfactual one.
- Using blame or morality when the scene is only about outcome comparison.
- Missing implied alternatives and treating the scene as plain success or failure.

## Minimal Pair
- Case: A student misses the deadline by one minute and thinks about how submitting on time would have avoided the zero. | Why: The emotion depends on comparing the actual bad outcome with a better possible outcome, which supports regret or disappointment.
- Case: A patient learns the test was negative after fearing it might be positive. | Why: The actual outcome is better than the feared alternative, so the emotion is relief.

## Boundary Stress Test
- If the character feels bad because they were careless, ask whether blame is driving the answer; if yes, exit to moral emotion.
- If the character is simply sad about a loss with no meaningful alternative, do not use this unit.
- If the scene mentions 'should have' but the question is really about shame or guilt, do not force a counterfactual reading.

## Generalization Note
This unit is about emotional contrast, not outcome quality alone: use it only when the comparison with an alternative outcome changes the emotion label.
