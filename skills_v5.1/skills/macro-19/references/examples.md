# macro-19 Examples and Boundaries

## Decision Variable
how to manage the image one presents to others

## Route Signal
The scene asks how to look, sound, or act in order to make the right impression in a formal or first-meeting context.

## Hard Boundary
- Apology and repair belong to macro-17
- Negotiation and compromise belong to macro-18
- Power-gap communication belongs to macro-20

## Shortcut To Avoid
- Do not over-read the scene as hierarchy whenever a polite style is simply impression management.
- Do not turn a self-presentation question into a relationship-repair question just because the setting is awkward.
- Do not solve by hidden emotion or truth inference when the question asks for outward presentation.

## Common Failure Modes
- Confusing polished presentation with submission to authority
- Treating a first impression question as apology or reconciliation
- Answering with internal attitude instead of outward conduct
- Ignoring audience expectations and giving a generic social answer
- Missing that the scene is about the image shown, not the private self

## Minimal Pair
- Case: A guest is meeting a teacher for the first time and wants to seem respectful and competent. | Why: The correct answer depends on self-presentation in a formal first impression.
- Case: A speaker must choose whether to apologize after accidentally insulting someone. | Why: This is relationship repair, so it belongs outside this macro.

## Boundary Stress Test
- A formal greeting is requested, but the real issue is whether the speaker is challenging authority.
- A polished email is requested, but the real issue is making a concession in a negotiation.
- A first meeting is described, but the main goal is to repair prior offense.
- The person wants to look confident, but the question asks what they actually feel.

## Generalization Note
This macro covers reusable impression-management scenes across introductions, etiquette, formal messages, and self-presentation choices, so a matching scene can route here directly even when a narrower micro diagnosis would also fit.
