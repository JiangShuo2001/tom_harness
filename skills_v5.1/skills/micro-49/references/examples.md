# micro-49 Examples and Boundaries

## Decision Variable
the amount of repair needed after relationship damage

## Route Signal
Route here when the prompt centers on mending strain after a social injury.

## Hard Boundary
- Not for deciding whether a remark was inappropriate; that is faux pas judgment.
- Not for deciding whether a secret should have been disclosed; that is privacy or boundary management.
- Not for bargaining over outcomes; that is negotiation.

## Shortcut To Avoid
- Do not answer from general niceness alone; ask whether repair is actually required.
- Do not confuse apology with admission of fault only; the core issue is relationship easing after damage.

## Common Failure Modes
- Treating the unit like offense detection instead of repair need detection.
- Confusing compensation or explanation with unrelated persuasion.
- Missing that a small repair can be appropriate even for indirect harm.

## Minimal Pair
- Case: Someone says a private detail in front of others, and the friend later feels exposed and hurt. | Why: The issue is what repair is needed after the relationship was damaged.
- Case: A speaker wonders whether a joke was socially inappropriate before anyone reacts. | Why: This is not repair after damage; it is judgment of the original act.

## Boundary Stress Test
- If the scene asks, 'Was this rude?' do not use this unit.
- If the scene asks, 'What should they do now to make it better?' use this unit.
- If the scene includes both offense and a proposed fix, score the repair need, not the original wrong.

## Generalization Note
This unit covers post-harm relationship maintenance across apologies, explanations, restitution, and reassurance; it does not decide whether the first act was wrong.
