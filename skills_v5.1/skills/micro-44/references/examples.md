# micro-44 Examples and Boundaries

## Decision Variable
whether the exchange or obligation is balanced and reciprocal

## Route Signal
Route here when the answer depends on whether one side has received more than it has given back.

## Hard Boundary
- Do not use for compromise design.
- Do not use for blame assignment.
- Do not use for social embarrassment.

## Shortcut To Avoid
- Avoid assuming every imbalance is exploitation.
- Avoid using apology language unless repair is explicitly asked.
- Avoid using trust history unless the scene is about reliability over time.

## Common Failure Modes
- Confusing fairness with kindness.
- Confusing reciprocity with loyalty.
- Confusing unequal roles with unfairness.

## Minimal Pair
- Case: A person repeatedly accepts help but never returns it. | Why: The question is whether the exchange is reciprocal.
- Case: A person must decide whether to offer a lower price to keep the conversation going. | Why: The question is about bargaining and deal design, not fairness judgment.

## Boundary Stress Test
- The scene includes a proposal for a new split: do not stay here if the question is what the best split should be.
- The scene includes an apology after harm: do not stay here if the question is blame or repair.

## Generalization Note
This unit covers norm-based reciprocity judgments across money, favors, effort, repayment, and burden-sharing without extending into deal-making or moral blame.
