# micro-20 Examples and Boundaries

## Decision Variable
the character's representation of another person's preference

## Route Signal
Use when the scene asks whether one person understands another person's likes, wants, or preferred option.

## Hard Boundary
- The target variable is another person's preference, not self preference.
- The answer should not depend on action purpose or belief revision.
- The skill is about representing taste or desire, not predicting the next move.

## Shortcut To Avoid
- Do not assume any mention of wanting automatically means intention; check whether the content is about liking versus doing.
- Do not collapse other preference into self preference when the same option appears for both people.
- Do not infer preference from emotional tone alone unless the scene explicitly links it to what the person likes or wants.

## Common Failure Modes
- Confusing another person's preference with the character's own preference.
- Treating a wish or desire as an action plan.
- Answering with the most likely action instead of the represented liking.

## Minimal Pair
- Case: Mina knows that Jordan likes spicy food, so she orders the spicier dish for him. | Why: The question is whether Mina represents Jordan's preference.
- Case: Mina herself likes spicy food and orders it for lunch. | Why: This is self preference, not understanding someone else's preference.

## Boundary Stress Test
- If the same person says both 'I want this' and 'I am doing this,' ask whether the question is about preference or intention.
- If two people prefer different options, verify that the task is about one person's model of the other's taste, not about conflict or negotiation.
- If the scene includes praise, habit, or repeated choice, check whether those cues are evidence for preference or for a separate goal.

## Generalization Note
This skill covers stable or momentary likes, wants, and choices as long as the central variable is one person's understanding of another person's preference.
