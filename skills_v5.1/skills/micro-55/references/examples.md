# micro-55 Examples and Boundaries

## Decision Variable
background probability or category typicality

## Route Signal
Use this micro unit when likelihood turns on priors: commonness, category frequency, ordinary typicality, or base-rate comparison.

## Hard Boundary
- This unit judges category likelihood, not approximate quantity language.
- This unit uses background probability, not sample-based posterior updating as the main operation.
- This unit does not assign causation, blame, responsibility, or moral fault.
- This unit does not infer hidden beliefs or intentions unless they are only evidence for a likelihood comparison.

## Shortcut To Avoid
- Do not choose the option that sounds most vivid, narratively interesting, or stereotype-matching while ignoring a stated base rate.
- Do not treat typicality as certainty; common outcomes can still be wrong in a particular case.

## Common Failure Modes
- Base-rate neglect: selecting a rare category because a description resembles it.
- Overcorrection: using the base rate even when the individual evidence is explicitly diagnostic.
- Confusing frequency with cause: assuming the commonest factor caused the event.
- Confusing vague quantity interpretation with probability comparison.

## Minimal Pair
- Case: A school has 800 students and 40 teachers. A randomly chosen person from the school parking lot is carrying a backpack. Who are they more likely to be? | Why: The answer depends on background frequency and typicality of roles, with no decisive direct evidence.
- Case: A teacher says that most students submitted the form. What approximate share submitted it? | Why: This is a near neighbor for micro-53, because the task is interpreting the vague quantifier most, not comparing category likelihoods from base rates.

## Boundary Stress Test
- If a rare-category description is vivid but non-diagnostic, keep the base rate central.
- If the scene says a random draw came from a population with known proportions, use those proportions.
- If the scene gives a new sample such as 8 of 10 observed cases, consider whether micro-54 is now primary.
- If a person directly saw the answer, do not override perception with typicality.

## Generalization Note
The unit applies beyond numerical puzzles: social roles, trust expectations, everyday risk, group membership, and likely behavior can all require base-rate or typicality reasoning when direct evidence is incomplete.
