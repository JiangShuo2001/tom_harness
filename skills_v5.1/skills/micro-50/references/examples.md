# micro-50 Examples and Boundaries

## Decision Variable
the desired impression others should form

## Route Signal
Use this micro unit when the scene asks how a person manages self-image, disclosure order, or self-description to shape others' impressions.

## Hard Boundary
- Privacy is about whether information may be shared; this unit is about how to present self-information once presentation is in view.
- Repair is about fixing harm after a social break; this unit is about image before or apart from repair.
- Power navigation is about strategy under hierarchy; this unit is about the image being projected, not rank-based tactics.
- Emotional display concerns visible feeling; this unit concerns the broader social picture of the self.

## Shortcut To Avoid
- Do not equate impression management with lying; truthful framing can still be self-presentation.
- Do not use secrecy as the default answer; secrecy may serve image, but the variable is the intended impression.
- Do not overfocus on single emotion cues; the unit is about overall self-image, not expression decoding.

## Common Failure Modes
- Confusing image management with privacy control.
- Treating apology or repair as if it were self-presentation.
- Assuming any selective disclosure is deception.
- Missing that disclosure order alone can be the diagnostic clue.

## Minimal Pair
- Case: A person introduces themselves by leading with leadership experience instead of a recent job gap. | Why: The choice is driven by the impression they want others to form from the self-description.
- Case: A person refuses to tell friends a confidential medical detail. | Why: This is primarily about whether the information should be shared, so it belongs to privacy and boundaries.

## Boundary Stress Test
- If the person could say the same facts in a different order and change the impression, this unit may apply.
- If the main goal is to protect a secret rather than shape a public image, this unit should not be used.
- If the scene adds apology, blame, or reconciliation, the center of gravity has moved out of this unit.

## Generalization Note
This unit covers bios, introductions, first impressions, selective disclosure, and framing choices whenever the primary variable is the impression others should form.
