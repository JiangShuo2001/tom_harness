# micro-41 Examples and Boundaries

## Decision Variable
the influence strategy directed at the listener

## Route Signal
Use this micro unit when the question asks which persuasive tactic, framing, or influence approach is best for changing a listener.

## Hard Boundary
- Not speaker motive.
- Not audience fit alone.
- Not negotiation or compromise.
- Not offense or blame judgment.

## Shortcut To Avoid
- Do not answer with the topic of the message; answer with the strategy used to influence.
- Do not confuse a polite tone with persuasion; politeness can be audience calibration or speaker motive.
- Do not treat any request as persuasion if the main job is to find a shared agreement.

## Common Failure Modes
- Confusing the speaker's reason for speaking with the tactic aimed at the listener.
- Choosing audience-calibrated wording when the real question is which argument changes the listener.
- Calling a negotiation scene persuasion because one side wants the other to agree.

## Minimal Pair
- Case: A parent chooses whether to explain the long-term health benefits or to promise a reward so a child will eat vegetables. | Why: The answer is about which influence strategy is most likely to change the child's behavior.
- Case: Two classmates discuss a proposal and each agrees to drop one demand so they can present a joint version. | Why: Near miss: both sides change terms to reach a shared plan, so the central variable is bilateral concession. Route to negotiation.

## Boundary Stress Test
- If the listener is not being asked to change, do not use this unit.
- If both sides must agree on terms, do not use this unit.
- If the question is only about making the message understandable or polite, do not use this unit.
- If the question is why the speaker said it, do not use this unit.

## Generalization Note
This unit covers any one-way influence attempt, including reasoning, emotional appeal, authority, framing, repetition, social proof, reassurance, or selective disclosure, as long as the central variable is the strategy used to change the listener.
