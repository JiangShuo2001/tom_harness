# micro-48 Examples and Boundaries

## Decision Variable
whether the information is within the disclosure boundary

## Route Signal
Use this micro unit when the central diagnostic question is whether information crosses a privacy boundary.

## Hard Boundary
- Does not handle repair after a boundary has already been crossed.
- Does not decide how to apologize, explain, or reconcile after disclosure.
- Does not judge overall self-image or audience impression.

## Shortcut To Avoid
- Do not treat every secret as a lie; a kept secret can be truthful withholding.
- Do not jump to repair just because the disclosure feels awkward; first ask whether the boundary was actually crossed.

## Common Failure Modes
- Confusing privacy with self-presentation.
- Confusing privacy with group membership or status.
- Using apology logic when the only question is whether to disclose.

## Minimal Pair
- Case: A person refuses to repeat a friend's private medical issue to a coworker. | Why: The answer depends on whether the medical detail is inside the disclosure boundary.
- Case: A person reveals a secret and then tries to smooth things over afterward. | Why: The privacy decision is already over; the real task is repair, so this unit should not be used.

## Boundary Stress Test
- If the audience is polite but not entitled to know, keep the information private.
- If the person asks for gossip, curiosity alone does not create permission.
- If the detail is flattering rather than harmful, it can still be private.

## Generalization Note
This unit covers disclosure boundaries across friendships, family, work, and online settings; the core judgment is always who may know what, not how to fix the aftermath.
