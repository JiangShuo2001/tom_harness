# macro-02 Examples and Boundaries

## Decision Variable
how one character understands another character's understanding

## Route Signal
The scene contains embedded belief, such as who thinks what about whom, or whether a fact is mutually known, privately known, or known to be known.

## Hard Boundary
- First-order false belief without nested belief
- Appearance-reality without model-of-mind structure
- Nonverbal social cue interpretation
- Literal pragmatics without epistemic nesting

## Shortcut To Avoid
- Do not jump straight to reality; preserve each character's represented knowledge state.
- Do not collapse second-order belief into the outer character's direct belief.
- Do not treat common knowledge as merely duplicated private knowledge.
- Do not use emotion or cue interpretation shortcuts when the question is about who thinks what.

## Common Failure Modes
- Answering from reality instead of the character's model of another mind
- Ignoring whether one character knows that the other was informed
- Flattening second-order belief into first-order belief
- Missing the difference between shared knowledge and one-sided knowledge

## Minimal Pair
- Case: A saw B leave the room before the object was moved, and A wonders where B will search. | Why: A must model B's outdated belief about the object's location.
- Case: Both A and B saw the update, and A predicts B's next move based on that shared awareness. | Why: The answer depends on common knowledge and A's model of B's knowledge.

## Boundary Stress Test
- If B knows the truth but A does not know that B knows the truth, this still belongs here if the question asks what A expects B to do.
- If the scene only asks where the person will search based on their own mistaken belief, it should route elsewhere.
- If the only twist is a misleading appearance with no modeling of another mind, it should not route here.

## Generalization Note
Treat this as a reusable prototype for nested belief and mutual awareness, not as a benchmark-specific trick; any scene with explicit mind-in-mind reasoning or shared knowledge can route directly here.
