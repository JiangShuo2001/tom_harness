# micro-21 Examples and Boundaries

## Decision Variable
which goal is superordinate versus subordinate

## Route Signal
Use this micro unit when a case asks how goals are nested or whether a goal is a means versus an end.

## Hard Boundary
- Not for goal conflict or decision making.
- Not for preference identification.
- Not for intention recognition.
- Not for next-action prediction.

## Shortcut To Avoid
- Do not treat the goal that gets mentioned first as the higher-level goal.
- Do not infer hierarchy from which goal feels more important unless the relation is explicitly asked.
- Do not use the final action as evidence of the top-level goal without checking the stated plan structure.

## Common Failure Modes
- Confusing a subtask with the main objective.
- Answering which goal the character will pursue instead of which goal is higher-level.
- Equating short-term with low value and long-term with preference without textual support.
- Merging goal hierarchy with intention or action prediction.

## Minimal Pair
- Case: A person buys ingredients so he can make dinner for his family. | Why: The question can ask which goal is the higher-level aim, with buying ingredients as a subgoal.
- Case: A girl writes a reminder so she will not forget the meeting. | Why: The reminder is a local means; not forgetting the meeting is the broader goal.

## Boundary Stress Test
- If two goals compete, do not rank them here; route to preference or commitment.
- If the story asks what the person will do next, do not answer hierarchy.
- If the only clue is that one goal happens earlier in time, that is not enough to prove it is higher-level.

## Generalization Note
Use this unit only for means-end structure among goals; it does not decide choice, value, or behavior.
